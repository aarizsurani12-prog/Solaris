The Pale Blue Dot ★ Highlight

The Giant of the Solar System

The largest planet — more massive than all other planets combined. Its Great Red Spot, a storm larger than Earth, has raged for centuries. Dozens of moons orbit this gas giant, including Europa, Enceladus, and Ganymede — each a potential home for life.

The Former Ninth Planet
